A dated selection from a larger catalogue

Title and object-type matches select records from the museum's published bulk export. Search operates across this captured selection; it is not a live search of every museum object. Missing dates and makers remain unknown. The source edition and our last check are different dates.

Images have their own rights

Small images are shown only when that individual media file has a recognized licence. Most are noncommercial Creative Commons images, with credit and licence beside each item. Metadata is not permission to reuse every image. Open the museum record to check the current terms before reuse. We do not harvest high-resolution or IIIF images.

Your saved objects

Saved object IDs stay in this browser. Search and comparison happen locally. Museum image hosts receive normal image requests; the shared CoolCode visit counter measures visits without receiving your search text or saved list.
